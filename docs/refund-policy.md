# Refund Policy

## Refund Window
Customers can request a refund within 14 days of purchase.

## Requirements
A receipt or order number is required. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

## How Refunds Are Paid
Refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 5 working days.

## Non-Refundable Items
Gift cards and custom-made items cannot be refunded.
